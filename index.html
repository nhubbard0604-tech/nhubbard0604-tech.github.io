You get a one-page breakdown: what arrived, when it arrived, and the top gaps in severity order, cart first, then welcome, then capture. Each finding is a fact from your test, not a generic checklist.

Step 3: Build in 7 days

If you want the fix built, I build Flow Foundation and have it live in 7 days for a flat fee, 50 percent to start and 50 percent on delivery. About 30 minutes of your time total for brand voice, offers, and access.
